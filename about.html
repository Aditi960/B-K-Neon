Our story

OUR CRAFT.

B.K. Neon started in Pune over 12 years ago with a single workshop, a roll of neon tube, and a belief that light has the power to transform any space. Today, B.K. Neon continues to create custom neon and signage work for businesses, shops, events, and interiors across different sectors.

The beginning

Where it all started.

It started in 2011, when B.K. Neon began working on local neon signage and custom sign boards for shops and businesses around Pune. What started as small local work slowly grew through referrals, repeat clients, and strong customer trust built through quality and durability.

Over the years, we expanded from traditional neon work into LED boards, acrylic signage, glow boards, flex printing, showroom branding, hospital signage, and other commercial signage solutions — adapting with changing industry trends while continuing hands-on fabrication work.

Today, B.K. Neon continues to create custom signage for shops, cafés, events, commercial spaces, and businesses across different sectors. Every project is still handled with a focus on strong build quality, clean finishing, durability, and practical custom work from our Pune workshop.
